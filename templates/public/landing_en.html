Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about the sales and inventory management system

What is the Azad business management system?

Azad is an integrated accounting and management suite for companies and retail stores, covering sales, purchasing, inventory, e-invoicing, account statements, cheque management and financial reports.

Is the software suitable for auto spare parts stores?

Yes! The system includes specialized features such as part-to-vehicle compatibility, 3 price levels (wholesale, semi-wholesale, retail), accurate stock tracking, professional invoices with your logo, and sales analytics.

How much does a subscription cost?

We offer 3 flexible plans for businesses of all sizes. All plans include technical support, free updates and training. See plan details

Does the system support e-invoicing?

Yes! The system fully supports e-invoicing. Create professional invoices in multiple designs, print in different sizes (A4, A5, thermal), add your logo and details, QR codes, and export PDF.

What AI features does the software offer?

The advanced AI in Azad provides smart market-based pricing recommendations, customer behavior analysis, sales forecasting, product performance analysis, hidden pattern detection, profit-boosting recommendations, a conversational assistant, and continuous learning from your data.

Can the software be used in multiple branches?

Yes! The system supports multi-warehouse and branch management. Track stock per branch, transfer goods between warehouses, and get separate reports per branch with centralized control.

How do I get started with Azad?

Getting started is easy! Just 3 steps: (1) book a free live demo, (2) choose the right plan, (3) start immediately! We provide free training for your team, legacy data import and ongoing support.

Is the system secure? Does it protect my data?

Security is our priority! We use full SSL encryption, automatic daily backups, intrusion protection, advanced user permissions, a comprehensive audit log and a secure card vault. Your data is protected with top global security standards.
