Terms of Service

Last updated: 2026-11-08 — a sample; the executed contract governs the specific deal.

Honest note: this page is not legal advice. Any binding relationship is governed by a signed contract executed by the parties. Full tax compliance in any country requires a contract and certificate from its authority — not from us.

1. Definitions

  • "Platform": AzadERP application and all its interfaces (web, printing, reports).
  • "Customer": the company or entity licensed to use the platform under an active subscription.
  • "Data": everything the customer enters into the platform, above all accounting entries.

2. License

  1. License grants internal use only; no resale or rental of the platform as a unit.
  2. Users/products/customers are governed by the agreed plan — not technically enforced, expected responsibility.
  3. No use for unlawful purposes or tax fraud/money laundering.

3. Privacy

Customer data belongs to the customer. Platform access is minimal and purpose-bound; no transfer to any third party without written approval, except where required by law.

4. Subscription and payment

  1. Renews annually unless the customer cancels before renewal after notice.
  2. Prices published on the pricing page; any increase subject to 30-day notice.
  3. Late payment allows suspension; records are not deleted due to delay.

5. Tax and compliance

Important: the platform technically supports tax settings, but does not grant compliance certification before any authority. Statutory filings require the authority's own contract and credentials — direct or via a certified provider. The platform stores data in submission-ready form.

6. SLA

Availability goals are published on the SLA page. Scheduled maintenance is announced in advance.

7. Liability

  1. The platform is not responsible for operational decisions made on analytics/forecasts.
  2. Maximum liability: the subscription fee paid for the current cycle.
  3. Limit does not apply where the law does not permit: gross negligence or wilful misconduct.

8. Termination

  1. Either party may terminate with 30 days' notice before period end.
  2. Upon termination, the customer may export data (CSV/PDF/print) before deletion.
  3. Account suspension for breach with notice is reserved to the platform.

9. Governing law

Governed by UAE law or the customer's law (as stated in the executed contract). Disputes: negotiation first, then arbitration.

Questions: contact us or via support.
