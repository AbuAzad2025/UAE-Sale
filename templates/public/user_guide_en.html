Quick Start

Welcome to {{ config.COMPANY_NAME }} - an all-in-one ERP for trade and auto spare parts businesses.

  1. Get an account: Contact us on WhatsApp {{ config.COMPANY_WHATSAPP }} to create your account and set your role.
  2. Log in with the username/password you receive, then change your password from your profile.
  3. Company setup: Owner Panel → Company Info (name, logo, VAT details), and tax/currency settings.
  4. Enter data: Products → Suppliers → Customers → then create your first sale or purchase.
Tip: The system is fully bilingual (AR/EN) and the smart assistant can walk you through any screen. Try typing "how do I create an invoice?" in the assistant.

Login & Roles

Security

  • Account lockout after repeated failed attempts.
  • Fine-grained permissions - costs and financial data are hidden from unauthorized roles.
  • Full tenant isolation - every user sees only their company's data.

Manage users under /users, and customize role permissions under Owner → Roles & Permissions.

Products Management

Add a New Product

  1. From the menu open "Products" then "New Product".
  2. Enter: name (AR/EN), part number, barcode, SKU, country of origin, category, brand.
  3. Set the three-tier pricing used automatically per customer type:
    • Regular price - for regular customers.
    • Merchant price - automatic discount for "merchant" customers.
    • Partner price - biggest discount for "partner" customers.
  4. Set cost price, current stock, low-stock alert level, unit, warranty, returnable flag.
Smart feature: Import products in bulk from an Excel upload, and ask the AI assistant to search market prices or suggest a price. You can also link each part to compatible makes/models.

Customer Management

Customer Types & Pricing

  • Regular: full price.
  • Merchant: merchant price (automatic discount).
  • Partner: partner price (largest discount).

Automatic Classification by purchase volume

  • VIP: purchases over 100,000 (in your base currency).
  • Premium: 50,000 and above.
  • Regular / Inactive: below that; inactive customers are flagged automatically.

Statement of Account

From the customer page you can view all invoices, payments, returns and the current balance, send the invoice via WhatsApp, and see the credit limit.

Supplier Management

  • Add / edit supplier details (name, phone, address, tax number, bank account).
  • View the supplier statement: invoices, payments and balance.
  • Supplier-specific purchase reports and quick search.

Sales & Returns

Creating a Sales Invoice

  1. Select the customer (balance, class and tier shown automatically).
  2. Select currency (AED, USD, EUR...) - exchange rate suggested automatically.
  3. Add products - price applied automatically per customer tier.
  4. Add discount and shipping; VAT applies when enabled.
  5. Choose payment method (cash/card/bank transfer/cheque/e-wallet) or credit.
  6. Save and print from the available templates (classic/modern/minimal/gulf) with company logo and VAT number.

Additional features

  • Returns: record a return from a sale invoice with automatic stock restore.
  • Archive / Restore old or cancelled invoices.
  • Approvals: large invoices can be routed for approval when workflows are enabled.
  • WhatsApp invoice delivery to the customer.
  • Convert a quotation to a sale (advanced modules).

Purchases

  • Create a purchase invoice from a supplier with currency and exchange rate.
  • Stock and cost price update automatically.
  • Print purchase invoices with your company header.
  • Convert a purchase order into an invoice (advanced modules).
  • Issue payment vouchers, view supplier reports and cost analysis.

Payments & Cheques

Receipt & Payment Vouchers

  • A voucher is generated for every payment with a sequence number, customer name, amount, currency and method.
  • Supported methods: CashCardBank transferChequeE-wallet
  • Enter payments from a sale or purchase directly; archive and restore vouchers.

Cheque Management

  • Track incoming/outgoing cheques: receive, deposit, clear, bounce, cancel.
  • Cheque alerts for due dates to avoid missed payments.
  • Full accounting integration with the ledger.
Security: card data is encrypted inside the Payment Vault and decrypted on-demand only with special permission.

Expenses

  • Record expenses (rent, utilities, fines, transport...) with categories.
  • Print expense vouchers and link them to ledger accounts.
  • Archive and restore vouchers.
  • Recurring expenses recorded automatically (advanced modules).

Ledger & Accounting

Full double-entry accounting system:

  • Journal entries (manual and tracked by document).
  • Chart of accounts (assets/liabilities/revenue/expenses).
  • Trial balance, income statement, balance sheet.
  • Vaults (treasuries) with cash/bank balance tracking.
  • Aging analysis for receivables and payables (30/60/90+ days).
  • Cash flow statement (operating/investing/financing).
  • Advanced ledger: customs & taxes, cheque integration, professional reports, financial ratios, trends and forecasting.

Warehouse & Inventory

  • Inventory dashboard with quantities and values.
  • Multiple warehouses and transfers between them.
  • Stock movements: full history of every in/out movement.
  • Low / out-of-stock alerts for quick reordering.
  • Manual stock adjustments and cost updates.
  • Stock takes and warehouse transfers (advanced modules).

Shipments & Inbound Shipments

Shipments follow the journey: send → arrive → start selling → close (or cancel).

Inbound shipments guide goods receiving: arrive → inspect → put-away → close (or cancel). Stock and cost update automatically at put-away.

Advanced Modules

  • Quotations - convert to a sale.
  • Purchase Orders - submit / approve / receive.
  • Fiscal periods - open / close / reopen.
  • Stock transfers - send/receive between warehouses.
  • Stock takes - count and approve differences.
  • Dunning - generate and send collection letters.
  • Recurring expenses.
  • Lots & Bins - precise batch and location tracking.
  • E-invoices - generate from local invoices.

HR & Payroll

  • Employees with a full UAE profile (ID, passport, visa, salary, allowances, IBAN).
  • Departments and employee distribution.
  • Leave management (annual/sick/personal) with balances, requests, approve/reject/cancel.
  • Payroll - generate → approve → pay, with housing/transport/phone allowances.

Approval Workflows

A multi-level approval engine with amount thresholds:

  • Example: sale above 10,000 AED → branch manager → owner.
  • Entities: sale, purchase, payment, expense and more.
  • States: pending → approve/reject with a full audit trail.
  • Configure workflows under "Approvals" and see pending requests on the dashboard.
