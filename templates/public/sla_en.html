Service Level Agreement (SLA)

Last updated: 2026-11-08 — operational targets; actuals measured from real incidents and tickets.

1. Monthly availability target

  • Target: 99.5% per month (no more than 216 minutes unplanned downtime/month).
  • Scheduled maintenance excluded; announced in advance.
  • Any breach beyond target: explained in writing within 48h of closure.

4. Backups

  • Automated backups per configured frequency (daily default).
  • Every backup is content-verified automatically (size, checksum, tables, data markers) — visible to the owner in the dashboard.
  • Retention: the 5 latest backups on-server; customer responsible for off-site copies.

5. Maintenance

  • Upgrades, security patches, migrations out-of-peak-hours.
  • User impact announced at least 72 hours ahead — via tickets, email, or the in-app banner.

6. Scope and exclusions

Not liable for outages caused by: customer network, misuse, unauthorised third-party access, or force majeure. These targets are operational intent — not absolute guarantees; this page does not modify the executed contract.

Applies to all customers regardless of plan. Enterprise/government deals may include a custom SLA in the contract. Questions: contact us.
