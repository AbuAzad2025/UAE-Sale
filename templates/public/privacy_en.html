Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-08 — plain-language, no decoration.

1. What we collect

  • Account data: name and email — only to operate the account and bill issues.
  • Usage data: page visits, technical errors — to improve the product (anonymised where possible).
  • Commercial data: whatever the customer enters into its account — the customer's; we inspect it only to fix a known fault.

2. No sale, no lease

We do not sell or lease customer data. No partner gets access unless strictly required for a chosen integration, under equivalent terms.

3. Where and how

  • Managed server with encryption at rest and in transit (TLS).
  • Card data is tokenised only; optional, never fully held by the platform.
  • Tax-authority credentials are sealed with Fernet at rest — never displayed or sent anywhere.
  • Every access follows the permission model; owner access is recorded in audit_logs.

4. Access from staff and developers

No employee has access to your data without a clear reason — staff permissions are minimal by default. Any audit failure is treated as a security defect, fixed urgently, disclosed openly to affected customers.

5. Your rights

  1. Access: export your data at any time (CSV/PDF).
  2. Correction: update or delete any record in your account, except where law requires retention (e.g., tax books).
  3. Deletion: deletion request honoured; operational data deleted within 30 days, subject to legal retention.
  4. Objection: disable analytics/marketing messages from the account directly.

6. Cookies

One required session cookie only. No marketing/analytics cookies; no behavioural tracking.

7. Changes

Material changes by 30-day advance notice via email and in-app notice before taking effect.

Questions: contact us — or via support. Sample; final policy is executed in each signed contract.
